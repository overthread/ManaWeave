How to play

Goal: bring the enemy weaver from 20 life down to 0. You are a weaver — the four threads hanging in your loom are your only resource.

Threads pay for everything. A card's cost is a row of coloured arrows (e.g. 🌋↑ 🌋←). Each arrow is paid by flipping one ready thread that matches both the colour and the direction. All your threads stand back up at the start of your round.

Weaving: threads start pointing up, so costs with sideways arrows (→ ↓ ←) must be prepared ahead — once per round, in the weave step, you may rotate one thread 90°.

Your round: draw a card → main step (play cards, move back-row units forward, attack — in any order) → optional weave step → end round.

Attacking: click the ⚔ badge on a front-row unit. That exhausts the unit and declares the combat — the damage itself is applied by hand (see below). Defender priority: enemy front unit in the lane first, else the back unit, else the enemy weaver.

The spell stack: cast spells don't discard — they wait on the shared spell stack (the strip under the battlefield). Reaction spells may be cast outside your own round, even during the opponent's turn — they go on top of the same stack. The newest spell (leftmost) resolves first; resolve or counter stack spells by hand, top-down.

By hand: the engine never resolves card effects or combat damage. Right-click any card, thread, deck or pile for a menu — deal damage, adjust stats, rotate/excite threads, draw, discard, move cards between zones. Adjust life with the −/+ buttons on either player bar. Manual actions are free: either weaver, any time.

Full rules reference: docs/game-design.md in the repository.
