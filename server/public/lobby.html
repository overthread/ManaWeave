Start the match

Each seat picks a deck (built in the Deck Builder). The host starts when both are ready.

How to play (rules summary)
  • Each weaver starts at 20 life with 4 threads in their Loom, all pointing up. Threads are your mana.
  • To pay for a card, exhaust (flip) threads — at least one must match the card's colour. All your threads ready again at the start of each of your rounds.
  • At the end of your round you may weave: rotate ONE thread 90° left or right. Some cards require an exhausted thread pointing a specific direction — weave ahead to unlock them.
  • Your round: draw a card → move units from back row to front row, play cards, and attack in ANY order → optional weave step → end.
  • Units deploy to any free slot (3 lanes × front/back). Only front-row units attack.
  • Attacking: enemy front unit in the lane fights back (mutual damage); an undefended back-row unit is hit without retaliating; an empty lane hits the weaver directly.
  • Units can't attack the round they're played (unless they have Quickstride).
  • Reduce the enemy weaver to 0 life to win. Drawing from an empty deck frays your pattern (1 life).
